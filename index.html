About Luigi

Luigi Marcelino Daniele is an evangelist with Come Expecting International, based in Pittsburgh.

My story

I came to Jesus two months after my wife (my girlfriend at the time) and I moved in together. At the same time, I had just landed my dream job selling exotic cars for one of the most prestigious brands in the world, a job I had been striving for over 10 years to get.

We were searching for a church we felt connected to. The day after an unsatisfying meeting at a local church, my wife noticed a YouTube video from Jonathan Shuttlesworth and sent it to me. Back then I had a three-hour daily commute, 90 minutes there and 90 minutes back. That was October 15, 2024, and from that day on I never stopped listening to him. I completely replaced secular music, because the Word of God was a magnet pulling on my soul.

Then he announced the launch of Revival Today Los Angeles for March 1, 2025. From the first meeting I wanted to give my life to Christ and be born again, but I didn’t want to be a hypocrite, because I was still living with my girlfriend and habitually committing sexual sin.

Two months later I decided I would no longer live with that conviction. One night I told her, “Let’s get married so we can live right by God and stop sinning.” With tears of joy, she said, “Okay.” I told her I would message Pastor Jonathan on Instagram and ask him to officiate our wedding.

The next day I sent him a detailed message explaining that I didn’t want to be a hypocrite, and that I wanted to be washed of all generational curses from my father’s side of the family. He told me, “Jesus never told his people to fix themselves and then go to him. He said come to me as you are. Get saved first, then I’ll marry you at the time of your choosing.”

So we did. I sent him that message on May 4, and on May 10, 2025, we gave our lives to Christ to have all our sins forgiven and our names written in the Lamb’s Book of Life. That was the day I received my first Holy Bible.

The call

I knew God had called me to preach during a 21-day fast where I only drank water and black coffee. It was day 14 and I was at work, and my flesh was screaming for food. I went into my office, closed the 20-foot tinted glass door, turned off the lights, sat down with my hands folded and my head bowed, and said, “Father, thank you for giving me the strength to complete this fast, and thank you for the revelation of what you want me to do to best serve you.”

When I opened my eyes, turned the lights back on, and opened the door, there was a $3 million car to my right, a $400,000 car in front of me, and a $1 million car to my left. Within 200 feet were maybe $20 million in cars, and I realized none of it meant anything to me anymore. I had lost all desire for cars or anything materialistic. That was the moment I felt in my spirit that God told me, “Now it’s time for you to go to Bible school.”
